Privacy policy

Waterline for iPhone. Effective September 27, 2026.

The short version

The developer collects nothing. There's no account with us, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code in the app. Your numbers come from your own Mac, either through an encrypted relay that can't read them or directly over Tailscale.

What the app handles, and where it stays

  • Your financial data (the transactions, budgets, subscriptions and account names your server holds) comes from your own Mac, over HTTPS, either through the relay described below or directly. It isn't sent anywhere else.
  • A copy of the last numbers is cached on your iPhone so the app opens instantly and works while your Mac is asleep. It's stored in the app's private cache with iOS Data Protection (unreadable while the phone is locked) and isn't included in backups.
  • A device key that lets your iPhone talk to your Mac, and the relay's encryption key if you paired through the relay, are kept in the iPhone's Keychain, on this device only. If you connect directly, your password is used once to get the device key and is never stored.
  • Settings (your server's address, the time scale you picked, whether Face ID lock is on, whether you allowed Chat) are kept in the app's preferences.
  • Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Disconnecting or unpairing in the app's Settings, or deleting the app, removes the cache, the keys and the settings. Disconnecting also revokes the device key on your Mac; unpairing removes your iPhone's access from the relay.

The relay

If you pair your iPhone with your Mac by scanning the code your Mac shows, the two meet at a small relay the developer runs on Cloudflare (waterline-relay). Your Mac seals a copy of your numbers with AES-256 encryption under a key that exists only on your Mac and your iPhone, and leaves it there; your iPhone fetches and opens it. Requests from your iPhone (filing a transaction, a sync, a Chat question) and your Mac's answers travel the same way, sealed.

The relay can't read any of it: it stores only the sealed data, which it can't open, plus the times it was uploaded, when your Mac last checked in, and scrambled (hashed) versions of the two devices' access tokens. It keeps one copy of your numbers at a time, replaced on every upload, and requests for at most a week. Unpairing in the app's Settings removes your iPhone's access; unpairing on your Mac erases everything the relay holds for you. Cloudflare's own handling of the requests it carries is covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Chat and third-party AI

Chat answers with Claude, Anthropic's AI model. Nothing is sent until you allow it: the first time you ask, the app lists what will be shared and asks for your permission. After that, each question goes to your Mac, and your Mac sends Anthropic the question together with your net income, this month's budget and spending by category, your active subscriptions and your last 40 transactions (date, account, merchant, amount, category), using the Anthropic API key set up on your Mac. You can turn Chat off at any time in the app's Settings. In demo mode, Chat never leaves your iPhone.

Your server

The server (Finance Agent) runs on your own computer, under your control. It connects to your banks through Plaid with read-only access, to the relay if you paired through it, and, when you use Chat, to Anthropic's Claude API with your own API key as described above. Those connections are between you and those companies, under their privacy policies: Plaid and Anthropic. The developer has no access to your server or anything on it.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly handle children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
