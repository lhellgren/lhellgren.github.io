Privacy policy

Waterline for iPhone. Effective September 28, 2026.

The short version

Waterline keeps your transactions and budget in your Waterline account so it can sync with your banks while the app is closed. It's read-only: it can't move money. Your data isn't sold, used for advertising or tracked across apps, and there's no analytics in the app. It goes to two other companies only: Plaid, which connects your banks, and Anthropic, only if you allow Claude to answer your questions and suggest categories. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Your account

You sign in with Apple. Waterline asks Apple for no name and no email address. Apple gives the app an ID for your Apple Account; Waterline's server stores only a scrambled (keyed hash) form of it, which is how it finds your account the next time you sign in.

What your account holds

  • Your transactions from the banks you link: date, merchant, amount, account and category, and whether each is still pending.
  • What's worked out from them: budgets, subscriptions, past months, and the categories Waterline learned for each merchant.
  • Your settings: take-home pay, savings rate, fixed costs and targets you enter, how often to sync, whether you allowed Claude, and your time zone.
  • Your bank links: the account names and institutions, when each last synced, and the access token Plaid issues for each link. Access tokens are encrypted (AES-256) before they're stored.
  • Signed-in devices: each device's name and when it was last used. Sign-in tokens are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.

Your account lives on Cloudflare, where Waterline's server runs, and is used only to show you your own numbers. It isn't shared with anyone else, and only you can sign in to it. Cloudflare's handling of the requests it carries is covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your banks and Plaid

Banks are connected through Plaid, with read-only access to transactions. You sign in to your bank in Plaid's own screens; Waterline never sees your bank username or password. Plaid then sends Waterline's server your transactions and account names. Plaid's handling of your data is covered by Plaid's end user privacy policy. Deleting your Waterline account also disconnects every bank link from Plaid.

Claude and third-party AI

Waterline can use Claude, Anthropic's AI model, in two ways. Nothing goes to Anthropic until you allow it: the app lists what will be shared and asks first. Once allowed:

  • Chat: each question you ask is sent to Anthropic with your net income, this month's budget and spending by category, your active subscriptions and your last 40 transactions (date, account, merchant, amount, category).
  • Categorizing: after each bank sync, the names and average amounts of new merchants Waterline's own rules can't place are sent so Claude can suggest a category.

Every figure is worked out by Waterline before anything is sent; Claude only explains them. Chat questions aren't stored in your account. Anthropic handles what it receives under Anthropic's privacy policy. You can turn Claude off at any time in the app's Settings; after that nothing is sent, and new merchants wait in Review for you to categorize. In demo mode, nothing leaves your iPhone.

Speaking a question

If you ask Chat out loud, iOS turns your speech into text on your iPhone. No audio leaves the phone; the text is then sent like a typed question, only after you tap Send.

On your iPhone

  • A copy of your latest numbers is cached so the app opens instantly and works offline. It's kept in the app's private cache with iOS Data Protection (unreadable while the phone is locked) and isn't included in backups.
  • Your sign-in token is kept in the iPhone's Keychain, on this device only.
  • Settings such as the time scale you picked and whether Face ID lock is on are kept in the app's preferences.
  • Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account keeps your data until you delete it. Settings > Delete account erases your transactions, budgets, settings and bank links from Waterline's server and disconnects your banks from Plaid. Signing out removes the cached numbers, token and settings from that iPhone and leaves your account as it is; removing a device in Settings signs that device out.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly handle children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
