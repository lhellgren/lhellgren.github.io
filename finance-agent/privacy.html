Privacy policy

Finance Agent for iPhone. Effective September 26, 2026.

The short version

The developer collects nothing. There's no account with us, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code in the app. The app talks only to the server you point it at, which is your own Mac.

What the app handles, and where it stays

  • Your financial data (the transactions, budgets, subscriptions and account names your server holds) comes from your server over an encrypted HTTPS connection. It isn't sent anywhere else.
  • A copy of the last numbers is cached on your iPhone so the app opens instantly and works while your Mac is asleep. It's stored in the app's private cache with iOS Data Protection (unreadable while the phone is locked) and isn't included in backups.
  • A device key that lets your iPhone talk to your server is kept in the iPhone's Keychain, on this device only. Your password is used once to get it and is never stored.
  • Settings (your server's address, the time scale you picked, whether Face ID lock is on) are kept in the app's preferences.
  • Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Disconnecting in the app's Settings, or deleting the app, removes the cache, the device key and the settings. Disconnecting also revokes the key on your server.

Your server

The Finance Agent server runs on your own computer, under your control. It connects to your banks through Plaid with read-only access, and if you turn on the chat feature, it sends your questions and a summary of your numbers to Anthropic's Claude API using your own API key. Those connections are between you and those companies, under their privacy policies: Plaid and Anthropic. The developer has no access to your server or anything on it.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly handle children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
