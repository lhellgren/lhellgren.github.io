Waterline

A private iPhone app for your own personal-finance server. Your budget, spending, subscriptions and accounts, straight from your Mac. No cloud service in between.

How it works

The server, Finance Agent, runs on your Mac: it syncs your bank accounts through Plaid (read-only), sorts transactions into categories and builds your budget. Waterline, on your iPhone, shows those numbers, lets you categorize what's left and answers questions about your spending. It talks only to the server address you give it.

Without a server, tap Explore with demo data to try every screen with an invented ledger.

Connecting your iPhone

  1. On the Mac, run the server and turn on Tailscale Serve: tailscale serve --bg 5151. It gives your Mac an https address like https://your-mac.your-tailnet.ts.net.
  2. Install Tailscale on your iPhone and sign in to the same account.
  3. In the app, enter that address and your dashboard password. The app swaps the password for a device key stored in your iPhone's Keychain and never saves the password itself.

Common questions

The app says it can't reach my Mac. The Mac is probably asleep, or Tailscale is off on one side. The app keeps showing the last numbers it loaded, with the time they're from, and refreshes when the Mac is back.

I lost my phone. On the Mac, open the dashboard, go to Settings > iPhone app and remove it. That phone can't connect again without your password.

Can the app move money? No. It only reads. Nothing in the app or the server can make a payment, transfer or trade.

What does Chat send, and to whom? The first time you ask, the app lists it and asks you. Your question goes to your Mac, which sends it with your budget, subscriptions and recent transactions to Anthropic's Claude, using your own API key. You can turn Chat off in Settings.

Is it financial advice? No. Waterline shows your own numbers and how they compare with the budget you set. It doesn't recommend investments or other financial products.

Where is my data? On your Mac, and a cached copy on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
