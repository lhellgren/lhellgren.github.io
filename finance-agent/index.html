Waterline

A private iPhone app for your own personal-finance server. Your budget, spending, subscriptions and accounts, straight from your Mac. No cloud service in between.

How it works

The server, Finance Agent, runs on your Mac: it syncs your bank accounts through Plaid (read-only), sorts transactions into categories and builds your budget. Waterline, on your iPhone, shows those numbers, lets you categorize what's left and answers questions about your spending. It talks only to the server address you give it.

Without a server, tap Explore with demo data to try every screen with an invented ledger.

Pairing your iPhone

  1. On the Mac, with the server running, open Terminal in the Finance Agent folder and run python scripts/relay_setup.py. It shows a QR code.
  2. In the app, tap Pair with your Mac and point the camera at the code.

From then on your Mac leaves an encrypted copy of your numbers on the relay whenever they change, and your iPhone picks it up, wherever either of them is. Only your Mac and your iPhone hold the key; the relay can't read what it carries (see the privacy policy).

Or connect directly over Tailscale: turn on Tailscale Serve on the Mac (tailscale serve --bg 5151), install Tailscale on your iPhone with the same account, and in the app choose Connect over Tailscale instead: your Mac's https://….ts.net address and your dashboard password.

Common questions

The app says my Mac last checked in hours ago. The Mac is asleep, or its dashboard isn't running. The app keeps showing the last numbers, and updates when the Mac is back. Anything you did meanwhile (filing a transaction, a sync) waits for it.

I lost my phone. Paired through the relay: run python scripts/relay_setup.py again on the Mac. That makes a new key and cuts off the old phone. Connected over Tailscale: on the Mac, open the dashboard, go to Settings > iPhone app and remove it.

Can the app move money? No. It only reads. Nothing in the app or the server can make a payment, transfer or trade.

What does Chat send, and to whom? The first time you ask, the app lists it and asks you. Your question goes to your Mac, which sends it with your budget, subscriptions and recent transactions to Anthropic's Claude, using your own API key. You can turn Chat off in Settings.

Is it financial advice? No. Waterline shows your own numbers and how they compare with the budget you set. It doesn't recommend investments or other financial products.

Where is my data? On your Mac, and a cached copy on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
