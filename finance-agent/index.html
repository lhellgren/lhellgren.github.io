Waterline

Your budget, spending and subscriptions on your iPhone, from your own bank data. Read-only: it never moves money.

Getting started

  1. Open Waterline and tap Continue with Apple. That makes your account; Waterline asks Apple for no name or email.
  2. On the Accounts tab, tap Add a bank and sign in to your bank in Plaid's screens. Add as many as you like.
  3. Your transactions come in within a few minutes. Waterline sorts them into categories and builds a budget from what you actually spend. Anything it can't place waits in Review: one tap files it, and the merchant is remembered.

Without signing in, tap Explore with demo data to try every screen with an invented ledger.

Common questions

A bank shows Reconnect. Your bank wants you to sign in again (banks ask every so often). Tap Reconnect on the Accounts tab or in Settings > Banks and sign in; syncing picks up where it stopped.

How often does it sync? Every 4 hours by default, even while the app is closed. Change it, or turn it off, in Settings > Syncing. Pull down on any screen, or tap Sync banks now, to sync right away.

Where are past months? On the Budgets tab, under History: each month by category, budget and week, and each year month by month.

A subscription is missing. Ones your bank data shows are found by themselves. For one billed somewhere Waterline can't see, tap Add a subscription on the Subscriptions tab.

I lost my phone. Sign in on your new phone with the same Apple Account, then remove the old one in Settings > Signed-in devices.

Can the app move money? No. Bank access is read-only; nothing in Waterline can make a payment, transfer or trade.

What does Claude see? Nothing until you allow it. Then Chat sends your question with your budget, subscriptions and recent transactions, and new merchants Waterline can't place are sent for a suggested category. Turn it off any time in Settings. See the privacy policy.

Is it financial advice? No. Waterline shows your own numbers and how they compare with the budget you set. It doesn't recommend investments or other financial products.

How do I delete my data? Settings > Delete account erases everything Waterline holds and disconnects your banks.
